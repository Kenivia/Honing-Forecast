# Market: don't try these

Warnings from finding population data for the gap (`Market.md`), 2026-10-25. Only read this when looking for new population or price sources.

- **Don't look for more stats pages from the gap.** The Wayback Machine has `/stats/generic` only for 03-31, 04-08 (no data in it), 05-20 and 06-23; archive.today has none; Common Crawl 2026-34 to 2026-43 has no lostark.bible stats pages.
- **Don't compare counts across lostark.bible's samples, only shares.** The combat power sample is a subset of recently active characters that shrank while the stats page grew: bards at 1760+ in it were 0.74 of the stats page's count on 06-29 and 0.42 on 10-25. Shares at or above X among 1700+ agree within 2 points.
- **Don't use the combat power samples across a big patch.** After 2026-10-21 the bard 1760 bracket fell from 601 to 293 in four days while the site rebuilt it.
- **Don't take an archived day with few classes as all classes.** Days with 1 to 6 classes swing by up to 3 points with which classes they are; only days with 20 or more are used.
- **Don't rebuild past spreads from character pages.** They keep a combat power history, not an item level history.
- **Don't use raid logs or lostark.bible's raid stats for population**: they only count characters that raid and log.
- **Don't use HF analytics gear for population**: it starts 2026-10-24, 55 characters, all HF users (`HF analytics.md`).
