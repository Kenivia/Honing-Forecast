# Hover tooltips

How the item tooltip is read. The scanner reads it in `crates/scanner/src/tooltip`, as the last step of every scan. The research behind it was done in Python (`scanner_setup/tooltips/tooltip_poc.py` and `video_report.py`), which still run and also cover the one thing the scanner does not do (matching the chest rows' icons).

The cases each rule was made for, the measurements and what was tried and dropped are in `Tooltips tried.md`. Read it only when trying something new here.

## What the scanner does with it

Each frame: find the title bar, read the title, find the "Amount Stacked" number and the bind lines, work out which slot is hovered, and write the amount and tradability onto that slot. If the tooltip has a chest panel, its rows are read too and matched against the game's own list of chests (see "Chests").

- **A window's header is not a tooltip.** A header is the title bar's flat blue-grey over a dark body, and with the window's name in the middle of it the part right of the name is the right length for a bar. A bar that ends at a located window's right edge on its header's rows is skipped (`WINDOW_WIDTHS`).
- **Where the bar is looked for** (`find_title_bar_wrapper` in `detect_title.rs` picks the regions, `find_title_bar` takes one):
  - With no window located nothing is looked for, and a hover still up counts as missed and retires as usual.
  - Otherwise the bound around the located windows: 440 px (1440p) to either side of them, from the top of the frame down to their last row of slots. A tooltip sits against a slot's column, level with it or pushed up.
  - A hover is first looked for at its last place, 8 px around its bar, and in the bound only if it is not there. Whatever is found first is kept, so this leans on there being no false bars (next bullet).
  - A run is measured past the region's sides, so one cut off by them is not taken for a bar, and a bar whose first row is the region's first row is not taken either, since its top may be above.
- **A bar is told by its whole width** (`SHORT` in `detect_title.rs`). Five rows in a row have to run the title's width, 16 px (1440p) short at most; rows above them only need half, and say where the bar starts. Half for every row would also fit a window's header with a tooltip over its right part: the visible piece is flat, blue-grey, over a dark body, and ends sharply at the tooltip's left edge. The nearest look-alike, a header cut off by the tooltip of its window's fifth column, is 40 px short of a title, hence 16.
- **Hover.** A tooltip that stays put and whose title bar has text in the same columns is one hover, kept in the scanner state across frames (it survives a few missed frames). The title check matters: tooltips pushed up by the screen bottom land in the same place for neighbouring slots, and without it two items' votes mix. Title, amount and tradability are voted on over the hover's frames.
- **Reading is deferred.** The scan only cuts the text lines out and sends them off (see "OCR runs elsewhere" in `Pipeline.md`); the votes are cast when the texts come back, which can be after the tooltip has gone. So the hover keeps what only its frame could tell (the bar, the slots beside it, where a chest goes), and a hover that is over stays in the state until its last texts are in.
- **A hover with its texts in but no slot waits for one** (`SLOT_WAIT_SCANS`), if it read a title or an icon of ours: the slots of a page just opened are recognised a few at a time, and with OCR quick its texts can be in before the slot under it is. It tries again on every scan and is dropped when the wait is up.
- **How much is read.** Title, amount, chest and description are each a read of their own, read until three agree, with one read of each kind out at a time: the next is sent when the last is back, so a hover asks for no more than OCR can deliver. A read that cannot be wrong settles its kind at once: a title with nothing over it that is a table's title to the letter, rows that are a chest's names to the letter with every count right (0.995 alike or more), a description that names a book. On top of that a title is read three times and then only when it looks different (the cursor moved over it), and amount and chest at most six times.
- **A tooltip with a chest panel has no title read.** A chest is told by its rows, and its title matches nothing.
- **The last frame.** While a read is out, the newest frame's lines are held. When the hover ends they wait for that read, and only go if it did not settle the matter. A short hover is over before its first read is back, and its first frame is the one most likely to be half faded in.
- **Which tooltips are read first** (`looks_hovered`). The game lights the hovered slot up, so a slot that still scores as its icon is not the hovered one. A slot counts as lit when it scores a little under the best it ever did as its icon (`best_score`); the cursor's tail over the slot below does the same, which errs on the safe side. A tooltip is of interest once any slot it can belong to (its column, level with it or below) is lit or has never been looked at. Until then its lines are held, since the highlight fades in: for up to `HOLD_SCANS` scans, or until the last scan of a screen gone still, after which nothing would send them. After that, or when it ends, they go behind everything else (`LOW_PRIORITY`). Scanning on for as long as lines are held is not an option: the extra frames scanned move which frames the slots are read on. The ones wrongly put behind are tooltips up for one scan, a slot whose first look was under the cursor, and stills, where a slot was never seen unlit.
- **Lines are cut on every frame and nothing more.** A line is an `OCRLine` (`ocr/jobs.rs`): the crop as captured, with the brightness estimate of its frame. The strip is made by whoever reads it (`Pipeline.md`).
- **Title.** The bar is split into text lines by hand and each line goes to the recogniser on its own, inverted to dark on white and resized to the 64 px line height the recogniser works at. The name is in the rarity colour and the "[X n]" count after it is white, so a coloured name is cropped to its coloured part and everything after it is skipped. The name runs until a word gap with no coloured pixel in it: a hyphen is too thin to count as a coloured column. Only the last "[X" is cut off as the count, since a name can have brackets of its own ("[Event] Metallurgy: Hellfire [11-14]"). This matters: with the count attached the recogniser returns "Pouch III [X" without the III, which is a different valid item. White (common) names keep the count and it is cut off as text. "(Bound)" is cut the same way.
  - **A pixel is coloured when its channels are a sixth of its brightest apart.** Ancient's pale tan is (227, 199, 168) at its fullest, and at a quarter its thin strokes had no coloured pixel.
  - **Each read of a hover's title is cut with another margin** (`TITLE_MARGINS`: 5, 4, 6 px at 1440p, in turn). The recogniser doubles a first digit at one margin and not at the next, as with amounts. On a lossless capture a tooltip standing still gives the same crop every time, which is one read however often it is sent, so re-hovering alone would not fix it.
- **Accepted titles** are the `title` values in `scanner_setup/templates/items.json`, the materials, compiled into the crate. A chest's title matches nothing and is not read: chests are told by their rows. A read has to be at least 0.9 similar (normalised Levenshtein) and 0.05 clear of the next title, and its numerals have to be the title's (next bullet); an exact read always counts. A hover's title is the canonical string from the table, or nothing.
- **Numerals** (`numerals`, `numerals_are` in `items.rs`). One wrong numeral barely changes how alike two names are ("Level 3" / "Level 4"), so numerals are compared apart. A name's numerals are the digits of every word that has a digit (the brackets of "[15-18]" come back as any of "[(I", and a space before a digit gets lost: "Level1") and, in titles, the words of roman letters only, all run together. A read's numerals are a title's when they are closer to it, in edits, than to those of every title that differs from it by numerals alone, and no more than a third of them are wrong.
  - So "(1l-14]" (digits 114) is "[11-14]" (1114) and not "[15-18]", and "3300,000 Gold Bar Pile" is the 300,000 one. A lone digit or "II" has to read right, since a wrong one is another title, and "?II" is as close to "II" as to "III" and matches nothing.
  - It is by rivals, not by a list of look-alike characters, on purpose: which misreads happen is not known ahead, which names they could be confused with is. The game has three book ranges; nothing here names them.
- **Bars of gold are accepted by title too** (`match_bar_title`), by the same rule, for a hover with no chest panel: the rows of `chests.json` that obtain all of one amount of gold or silver and can sit in a slot. See "Bars of gold" under "Chests".
- **Amount.** The number is the yellow run at the end of the "Amount Stacked" line. The recogniser doubles or drops a digit when the crop is one pixel wider or narrower, and which crop does it differs from number to number. So the number is read from two plain crops with different margins, and when they disagree a third crop built from the yellow colour alone breaks the tie; with no two agreeing the frame gives no amount. Compression can take the yellow out of a thin last digit, so the line may run a little past the yellow as long as what follows is not white.
- **Which line is "Amount Stacked".** A candidate is a line with a white label and yellow up to its end. The stacked line is the first of a pair one line apart (stacked, then total) whose number starts where "Amount Stacked: " ends, else the first candidate whose number starts there; else the frame has no amount. The number starts 169 px in at 1440p (`STACKED_NUMBER_LEFT`) and 205 to 213 after "Total Amount Owned: ", both beyond the cursor's reach; 10 either way is allowed. English only. Other lines pass as candidates too, with the number elsewhere ("Owned Phoenix Plume(s) x1529", lines above the amounts on some chests), which is why the number's start is checked.
- **Which book.** Books of one kind share an icon and differ by honing range, so the slot icon cannot say which one a stack is. The tooltip can, twice over. Its title has the range. Its description has a line per level ("Honing Lv. 11 (1,645): +10%") and the gear ("Determination of Destiny Weapon"), which `item_from_body` turns into the `body` key of an `items.json` row ("Weapon 11-14"). A frame that lost a line gives a range no row has, and so no vote. The level is every digit before the bracket: "11" is drawn wide and comes back as "Honing Lv. 1 1 (1,645)" on every read. The description is the better source: the cursor covers the start of a long title whenever the tooltip is level with its slot, while the description is 200 px lower. A pushed-up tooltip is the other way round.
- **When the description is read** (`read_body`). Only for a hover whose large icon is closest to one that several materials share (`shares_icon`), or whose voted title is that of bars whose gold is bound in different ways (`bars_differ`), as a fourth kind of read beside title, amount and chest: up to ten tan or pale yellow lines between the icon and the amount, over the whole width, until three reads agree. `described_by` turns the lines into votes (`Described`): a book's levels settle it as its material, and the hover's `label` is that, else the title's row's. A bar's go to the bars of its title whose descriptions are closest ("Bars of gold").
- **Tradability** is "Tradable" only when no red right-aligned line is found *and* no lines were merged into one block above the amounts. A cursor beyond its usual reach merges the bind line and "Untradable" into one block.
- **A chest's tradability also comes from the game's table.** When no tooltip gave one, a slot takes the one way every chest it can be is bound (`bound_alike`, on `binds` of `chests.json`; `read_status` in `scan_result.rs`): the chests read for it, else the chests of its icon. Character bound is left out in roster storage, where the game does not allow it. A tooltip's own read always goes first. So a chest whose icon already says what it opens to is good unhovered instead of `NeedTradability`. What is left is the Destiny Shard Pouches, which come tradable too. Materials still need the tooltip or the assumed tradability.
- **Icon.** The tooltip's large icon is compared with every material's slot icon template (`tooltip_icon.rs`; not the chests', which are told by their rows), voted on like the rest until three frames agree, and only frames where it leads the next template by the margin vote: the two books differ by an emblem and never lead. It is cut out at its fixed place under the title bar, resized to the templates' 64 px, and scored as the difference per pixel over the closest 75% of pixels at the best of nine one-pixel shifts. The pixels left out absorb the cursor and the background, which is not a slot's. The limits are 54 (summed over the three channels) and a lead of 12. It is skipped on a frame whose layout has a chest panel, and a hover gets six tries at most: a chest's icon matches nothing and a book's never leads.
- **Hovered slot** (`hovered_slot`). The candidates are the remembered slots that hold the tooltip's icon, in the column the tooltip sits against, level with or below it. In order:
  1. The only one. Not when it never lit up during the hover (`Hover.lit`, from `looks_hovered`) and its own count is another number than the tooltip's ("9999", which is 9999+, is none): then the hovered slot is one that matches nothing.
  2. The one level with the tooltip, unless other candidates were seen lit and it was not. A pushed-up tooltip lines up with some row now and then, and that row can hold the same icon.
  3. The only one whose own count is the tooltip's (a slot with no number counts as 1); of several, the only one of them that was lit.
  4. The only one no other tooltip was tied to with another amount: for two stacks of one chest whose own counts misread under the cursor, once the other stack's tooltip is tied to it, this one is what is left.
  - **Being lit picks nothing by itself.** The highlight takes a few scans to fade on the slot the cursor came from, and the cursor's tail lights the slot below.
  - The icon is the one the title is drawn with, else the one of the material the description named, else the one matched from the picture; for a chest it is any icon of the chests its rows read as, against any icon the slot may be (`Pipeline.md`). A tooltip with none of them never resolves to a slot.
- **A tie is taken back when a rival turns up** (`untie_rivals` in `slots.rs`). The cursor hides the hovered slot, so a slot never seen without it is no candidate, and another stack of the same thing in its column can get its tooltip, the more so as the cursor's tail lights the slot below. So a slot keeps, with what a tooltip wrote, the icons that tooltip's item is drawn with and the slots it could have sat against that showed none of them then (`passed_over`). When one of those is later recognised as one of those icons, the first slot loses what the tooltip wrote and any chest loses it as its slot: it needs hovering again, as does the new one. The user is not done before every slot was seen unlit, so this always gets its chance.
  - It goes by the newly recognised slot's own icon, not by what it may also be: alternatives come and go between looks.
  - The cost: a stack hovered while another of its kind further down its column was still unread is hovered again.
- **Which page.** The slots beside a tooltip are those of the page last lit in that window (`tooltip_page_num`), not only of a page lit right now: the tooltip of a slot in the top rows is tall enough to cover the tabs of its own window. The page is given up when another tab is lit, the window is lost, or its own tab has looked dark on three scans in a row while no tooltip was over it. Slots are still only read while a tab is lit.
- **Result.** `hover` in the scanner state has the voted title, icon, amount, tradability and slot. The slot gets `hovered`, `tooltip_amount`, `tradability` and `label`, which it keeps while it holds the same icon. A hover only writes what it read: a tooltip seen for a frame or two while fading in has no tradability and often no amount, and must not blank what a longer hover of the same slot wrote. The slot also gets `tooltip_failed` when the hover is over (or out of reads) with no amount: no amount line found, or no two crops ever agreeing. A slot the user edited is not written to. The slot's own OCR'd count is left alone; the tooltip amount is the full number where the slot shows 9999 or is misread.

Colours are compared after brightness normalisation, so the constants in the Rust code are a few levels below the on-screen values quoted below. Pixel constants there are 1440p and scaled by the scanner's scale factor like everything else; the sizes in this note are the 1080p measurements, which are 3/4 of them.

`cargo run --release --bin tooltip_test` is the native harness: it runs the whole scanner over stills, or over a recording piped in as raw frames by `scanner_setup/tooltips/dump_frames.py`, and prints each hover and every slot that got tooltip data. `pnpm scanner-suite` runs it over every recording and the stills folders and compares two runs (`Browser harness/Screen scanner.md`).

## Chests

A chest is one of `scanner_setup/templates/chests.json`, the game's own list of everything that opens to a material, gold or silver (`Game files.md`), and its tooltip's rows say which. Its title is not used: OCR loses a numeral alone on the second line, and one title is several chests anyway. Its icon is not needed either. The structs (`Chest`, `ChestKind`, `ChestContent`, `Variant`) live in the scanner crate and in TypeScript, not in core.

- **Rows.** Text lines are taken from just right of the icon column. Lines less than 10 px apart (1080p) belong to one row; the last line of a row is the "xN" count and the ones before it the name. Rows end at the first gap over 22 px, because over a dark background the body seems to run on into the shortcut hints.
- **Reading.** Each line goes to the recogniser on its own with 4 px of margin across and 2 px down, never reaching left of the text start. That margin is tuned: a wider one lets the icon's edge in and the read collapses, and white padding around the line is worse too. Every row's count is read, known item or not.
- **Which chests list these rows** (`chests_listing`). A chest fits when every row is a content of its own: the name at least 0.8 alike (normalised Levenshtein, lower case, "(Bound)" cut off), the count the same. A chest with contents that differ by class may have up to `extra` rows that are none of its contents. Every chest of the table is tried. Those whose contents are all there go first.
- **A tooltip that does not list everything.** When no chest is all of the rows, the chests the rows are part of count instead, but only those a tooltip cannot list whole: a `by_class` chest, whose rows depend on the class, with any number of rows, and a chest with more than `ROW_CAP` (10) contents when at least 5 rows were read. The tooltip stops at 10 rows, and when it reaches the bottom of the screen its last row is under the "Scroll" hint. A read that is only part of a chest never settles the hover in one go. Any chest fitting in part is wrong: one row is in many chests, and 0.8 alike is loose for a single row.
  - **What is left**: battle item chests that are not in the table and list 8 to 10 of the 28 things in "Battle Item Selection Chest" are stored as that one. It is irrelevant, so the cost is a line in the chest list; a slot of its art (`shop_icon_31@epic`, which the fusion material chest shares) in that column with the same amount would be marked irrelevant.
  - Whether the tooltip reaches the bottom of the frame is not looked at.
- **Digits tell rows apart, roman numerals are not read.** A row's digits have to be the title's by the rule for titles ("Numerals" above), against the titles that differ by digits alone: "[16- 191" is "Phantom [16-19]" and not "[13-15]", "(5-7)" is "[5-7]", "Level 1" is not "Level 2". A roman numeral at the end is another matter: the body font draws it as bare strokes, which come back as anything ("II", "I", "?I", "!", "VI", "IV", glued to the word before it or not), so which numeral it is cannot be read off that.
  - **What is done** (`tiered` in `chest.rs`). A title that ends in a roman numeral is its stem and then anything: whatever the name ends in, up to one more character than the longest numeral of the table, stands for the numeral, and the rest is compared with the stem. A name with nothing left over is two edits short. So every tier of a chest scores the same, a row with no numeral read still fits (worse than a title without one), and "Pouch!" fits "Pouch I". Nothing lists which characters a stroke can come back as.
  - **What ignoring the numeral costs**, over the whole table: with exact titles no two chests that hold different things list the same rows. Knowing only that there is a numeral makes one group of three alike ("Adventurer's Path Chest II" to "IV", which hold each other). Not even knowing that makes eight, three of them with a chest that counts. The rule above is between the two: presence is not required, but a name that ends in something scores better against a title with a numeral.
- **The header only breaks ties.** The wording of the header's yellow phrase varies too much to give the kind. The chest that fits has its kind in the table. When chests of several kinds fit, those of the header's kind go first. "Obtain [one] of the following items at random" has the same one short word as "Obtain [all]", so a header read as obtain all lets random chests through too; which of them it is, the slots then say.
- **No yellow phrase, no chest panel.** The phrase has to be 8 px wide (1440p; "all" is 13). A few yellow pixels of an item's gold border showing through a black stretch of the body must not pass for a panel, since a tooltip with a panel has no title read.
- **Votes.** Each read votes for the chests that fit best, all of them when several fit alike. Reading stops once one has three votes, or after six reads. The chests with the most votes are the hover's.
- **Narrowing by the slots.** Chests that list the same things can be drawn differently. Of the voted ones, those drawn like the slot the tooltip is level with are kept, else those drawn like any slot in its column, if any are.
- **What is stored** (`Chest`): the ids left (`variants`), their titles for display, the first one's kind and contents, the slot icons they are drawn with, the stacked amount, the tradability, the column and the slot. Several ids are usual and harmless: 8 chests list "Artisan's Metallurgy: Level 1 x1, Artisan's Tailoring: Level 1 x1". It only matters when they hold different chests inside, which the manifest checks (`Manifest.md`).
- **Irrelevant chests are read like any other.** The table also has the chests that are drawn like one that counts and open to nothing counted (`Game files.md`). One with an item level across its slot counts for the slot reader only from the lowest level a chest that counts asks for (`has_level` in `items.rs`): older ones let a chest that counts pass as their look-alike art. One is voted on, narrowed by the slots and stored the same way, with `irrelevant` set when every chest it can be is; its slot then shows as irrelevant (`Slot grid.md`). When the chests with the most votes are of both sorts it is not irrelevant, and the manifest lists it under "Not included".
- **A tooltip of no chest in the table is not stored.** That is a chest drawn like nothing in the table (its slot matches no icon anyway), one the client has no contents for, or a misread.
- **Storing.** From the first read that fits, because the browser only gets one or two frames of a hover; later reads of the same hover replace it.
- **Which chest it is.** The tooltip's column gives the inventory, page and column. The slot is the one the hover resolved to, else the one the tooltip is level with when it was not pushed up, unless that slot holds something else: a pushed-up tooltip lines up with some row about once in 15. A chest's slot is the one its hover resolved to whenever it has one, over one an earlier read of the same hover only took for being level: with slow OCR the rows are back before the amount that picks the slot. A new read replaces the chest last seen in that slot, else one in the same column that can be the same chest (an id in common) with the same tradability and amount (a missing one matches anything), which is then narrowed to the ids both reads have; else it is added.
- **Level or pushed up.** A pushed-up tooltip's shortcut hints end at the screen bottom. The hints box is 80% black and is followed by comparing its sides with the background just outside it, which only works where that background is bright. Over a dark one (the storage NPC's dialogue bar) the test says nothing and alignment with a slot row decides alone.
- **The title bar's top** is found up to 5 px low when something is over its first rows; its bottom is not. The slot test counts the top back from the bottom using the two known bar heights.
- **Bars of gold.** A bar ("10,000 Gold Bars", "50,000 Gold Chest", "300,000 Gold Bar Pile") opens without a choice and its tooltip has no chest panel, so no rows vote, its title is not a material's and its large icon is not compared with chest icons. So a hover with no rows read is the bars of its title (`bars_titled` in `hovered_chest`), narrowed by the slots like any chest and stored the same way, with no rows. That also says which bar a slot is where one icon is several amounts (ancient `use_13_36` is 50,000, 75,000 or 100,000). A title that never reads leaves the bar where it was.
  - **Which of a title's bars, by the description.** Bars of one title, art and rarity can hold gold bound in different ways (`Game files.md`), and "Thick Gold Bar (Bound)" reads as "Thick Gold Bar" once "(Bound)" is cut. For those titles the description is read, all its lines joined, and compared with each bar's `descs` by normalised Levenshtein, lower case and spaces squashed. The closest bars vote when they are 0.8 alike or more, and 0.995 settles the read. `hovered_chest` keeps the bars with the most votes. Plain and deposit bars are one row, both tradable, so they need not be told apart.
  - **A bar read before its description** keeps all the bars of its title, and its slot asks to be hovered again (`unread_bar` in `amount_status`) rather than counting as read: the manifest would not know its band. The description is only asked for once the title is voted, so a hover over within a scan or two of its title read gets none.

`cargo run --release --bin chest_rows -- "<name> | <count>" ...` prints the chests some rows read as, for trying a read by hand.

## What a tooltip looks like

Top to bottom:

- **Title bar.** Fully opaque, one flat colour (30, 34, 39), 307 px wide. 34 px tall with a one-line title, 52 px with two lines. The item name is in the rarity colour, followed by a white `[X n]` that can wrap onto the second line. Above the text is a clean strip of the bar colour, 11 rows. A single-item stack has no `[X n]` at all.
- **Body.** About 97% opaque over (14, 19, 20), so its pixels sit 0 to 8 levels above that colour depending on what is behind.
  - Item icon: 64 x 64, the native icon art, 7 px in from the title's left edge and 17 px below the title bar, over a rarity gradient. Rarity and "Item Tier n" text to its right.
  - Optional right-aligned white line ("Required Item Lv. 1640").
  - Bound items only: a white left-aligned "Bound to Character" / "Bound to Roster" line, then a red right-aligned "Untradable". Tradable items have a white right-aligned "Tradable" instead and no bind line.
  - Description lines in tan (228, 204, 130), with phrases some descriptions highlight in pale yellow (255, 255, 172, the game's `#FFFFFFAC`). "Consumed before tradable items." is in the bright amount yellow.
  - "Amount Stacked: n" and "Total Amount Owned: n": white label, number in opaque (255, 213, 0), with a thousands separator.
  - Red restriction lines, then cyan acquisition sources.
- **Chest contents** (boxes and pouches): a pure black (0, 0, 0) panel inset 5 px in the body, placed after the restriction lines. A header with one yellow phrase, then one row per item: a 30 px icon square 18 px in from the title's left edge, the name in rarity colour, and a white "xN" under the name.
  The header comes in three kinds: "Obtain [all] of the following items." (one short word), "You can [select and obtain 1] of the following items." (white text after it on the same line), "There is a [chance that you can obtain one] of the following items." (yellow runs to the end of the line). "Obtain one of the following items at random" also has one short yellow word.
- **Shortcut hints.** A separate, narrower box below, 80% black. Its lines depend on where the item is; nothing useful is in it.

All sizes are 1080p pixels and scale with the UI height. Colours are at the brightness of the 1080p examples.

## Placement

The tooltip sits against the hovered slot's column: its title starts 8 px right of the slot's right edge, or ends 12 px left of the slot's left edge when it flips to the left. Its top equals the slot's top, unless the screen bottom would cut it off, in which case it is pushed up and only the column is known. Most examples are pushed up.

## The cursor

Screenshots do not include the cursor, the recording does. It is customisable, so it is never a matching target. It sits over the hovered slot and, when the tooltip is on the slot's right, pokes into the tooltip's left edge. At the largest size it reaches about 90 px in: the left of the title bar, most of the icon when the tooltip is level with the slot, and the start of the bind and amount labels are covered. The hovered slot itself and its hover glow (a brighter inner ring about 6 px wide) are usually covered and must not be relied on. Whatever the cursor touches merges with it when the body is split into lines, so the header and amount lines are read only from columns 100 px and beyond.

Tooltips also fade in over a few frames, during which no colour matches.

## Known limits

- **Reading depends on the brightness estimate being right to a few settings.** Text colours are normalised like everything else (the setting does change them), so there is no shortcut around the estimate; it is within a setting at 1080p (`Pipeline.md`).
- **Colour limits are by hue where they can be.** After normalising with the gamma law, a text colour's channel ratios are the same at every setting and on soft edges, to within 0.02: amount yellow has green at 0.82 of red and blue at 0; description tan 0.89 and 0.54; red 0.39 and 0.37; white and grey 1. How bright a text pixel is depends on how much of the glyph covers it (110 to 255, median 150 to 200) and drops a few percent towards setting 100. So yellow is told by its ratios on any text pixel. Its blue limit is half of red: compression washes thin yellow out (green up to 0.95 of red, blue up to 0.4), and tan is only just beyond at 0.54. A description line cannot pass for an amount anyway, having no white label.
- **The chest panel's black is 6 or less.** It is 0 on screen. The body around it normalises to 12 to 20, and a higher limit lets the panel start among the amount lines at bright settings.
- **Across brightness settings**, title, amount, tradability and contents read at every setting but 5. At 5 the title bar and the body are one level apart in every channel, which is where the two cross over. On a compressed recording at setting 0 no tooltip is found: compression leaves the dark panels as noise between 0 and 7.
- **Where the title bar ends** is found in the captured colours, as three rows in a row that differ from the bar by a quarter of its level (at least 2). Done on normalised colours it fails below setting 50, where bar and body are a few levels apart and normalising multiplies that along with the noise. The body's own colour is likewise checked after taking off up to 9 captured levels of what shows through.
- **A tooltip that is only up for a few frames of a compressed recording sits at the edge of several colour tests**, and which side it falls on moves with the brightness estimate, because the brightness lookup rounds neighbouring levels together at places that shift with it. The title bar's blue-grey test (blue minus red) has its limit at 2 for that reason (other windows' grey bars measure -1 to 1). Not seen on tooltips that stay up.
- **A chest can be listed twice**, once without its amount, when a brief hover's read lands after a longer one's.
- **A wrong slot is possible when the hovered slot was never seen**, until it is: the cursor hides the hovered slot, and if another stack of the same item sits lower in the same column, that one can get the tooltip's data. It is taken back when the hidden slot is recognised ("A tie is taken back when a rival turns up"). What is left is a slot that is never recognised at all (no template) beside a stack that shows the tooltip's amount or was lit by the cursor's tail.
- **Two stacks of one item in a column** resolve only if the tooltip is level with one of them, their own counts differ and were read right, or they show the same count and only one of them lit up.
- **The Decay and Phantom books** (purple, "[13-15]" and "[16-19]") have no template and no row, so they are irrelevant slots.
- **Chests that share an icon** (II and III of the same pouch) are one slot template. Their rows tell them apart, so each needs its own hover unless every chest the icon can be opens alike.
- **H.264 recordings break exact colours.** The scanner matches the title colour with a tolerance of 8 either way. Live screen share is 4:2:0 but otherwise uncompressed (`Browser harness/Screen scanner.md`).
- **Character against Roster bound** is told apart only by how far the bind line extends (English text). When the cursor hides the line the frame casts no vote.
- **Titles and row names are English only.**
- The recogniser returns `?` for characters it does not know, the bracket of the count among them.
- The chest rows' icons are not read by the scanner.
- **A chest row hidden by the cursor fits no chest**, since every content has to be a row; the frame then casts no vote.
- **A count given as a range** ("x100-2,000", 66 boxes) fits no chest: only the lower count is in the table.
- **A chest tooltip seen for a frame or two can be stored without its amount**, and its slot then shows an error until it is hovered again.
- **A pushed-up chest tooltip over a dark background can get the wrong row** if its top happens to line up with a slot row (about 1 in 15), and would then replace the chest stored for that slot.
- **Two stacks of the same chest in one column** with the same amount, or one read before its amount was, are stored as one.
- **Tooltip reading costs OCR lines, not scan time**: a scan with a chest tooltip takes as long as one with an item tooltip, but a chest's rows are 8 or 9 more lines to read, and a burst of them is what makes a hover's first read late.
