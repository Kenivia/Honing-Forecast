# Choices

The character's Choices sub-page (`/:char/choices`, under Income in the sidebar): for each choice, what taking an option is worth to this character, in average gold under its plan, and a button that adds the option to the budgets. The choices are the raids' bonus chests (nothing, or the chest's materials for its gold) Paradise's rewards ("Paradise" below) and one the user makes ("Custom" below).

Code: `Components/Character/Choices/` (`Choices.vue` the page, `Paradise.vue` its Paradise tab, `Custom.vue` its Custom tab, `ChoiceJobs.ts` the runs), the choice type, worth text and budget writes in `Utils/Choices.ts`, the data in `frontend/Data/bonus_chests.json` (written by `scanner_setup/game_files/bonus_chests.py`, `Game files.md`, "Bonus chests") and `frontend/Data/paradise.json` (`scripts/game_files/paradise.py`, `Game files.md`, "Paradise"). `tests/choices.test.ts` covers the gold taken, the chests across tiers (T4 for Serca, special leaps included; Serca for T4), the difficulty shown, the raid order and the Paradise data's shape; `tests/unit/income-choices/choices-page.test.ts` the page, the Paradise tab and the Custom tab.

## A choice

`Choice` is a chest without a count: `id`, `group`, `title`, `band` and `options`, each a bag of label to amount, a cost being negative, and optionally `difficulty` and `item_level`. `others` is what else it gives, shown on hover. `names` says what each option is where the options are not the choice itself, and `wealth` is Paradise's options with Wealth. `PRESET_CHOICES` is the bonus chests and `PARADISE_CHOICES` Paradise; the user's is a `CustomChoice` (band, names, options) per roster, made a `Choice` by `as_custom`.

**A character sees the raids its item level reaches, whatever its tier**: a raid is listed when its lowest difficulty is at or under the character's achieved item level (`Choices.vue`, `raids`). Difficulties are locked by item level, not tier, and a T4 character can be 1730 or more, so T4 and Serca are treated alike; tier only changes what a chest is worth. The options go through `process_chests` like any chest: a Serca character's T4 materials become Serca ones at the ratio (T4 special leaps a fifth, as Serca special leaps, which Rust adds to the free-tap budget), and a T4 character's Serca materials are dropped, leaving the gold and shards, so such a chest is worth little or less than nothing.

## What an option is worth

- **The run without** (`base_job`): the budgets now (`snapshot_of`, as Income's "now") with the character's and roster's chests, optimized, then its histogram.
- **A run per option that is not empty** (`option_job`): the same with the option as a one-option chest of count 1 (`as_chest`), so it is taken; an empty option is the run without. It goes on from the optimizer state of the run without (`states_payload`), which the optimizer takes as a plan to go on from (`Calculator core/Optimizer.md`, "Chained runs"), and is queued only once that is done.
- **Worth** is the option's metric of the user's plan (`metric_per_plan[1]`) less the run without's: positive means take it. Each option is against the budgets as they are, alone, so two gates are not taken together.
- Results are kept for the session by the hash of the payload without its seed; a budget change makes new hashes and every run again. The runs go through `WasmInterface/OptimizerJobs.ts` (`Income.md`); leaving the page cancels them. About 30 s for 35 rows of a small plan; the three raids shown take a few seconds.
- What the metric sees of the gold: the chest's gold is negative in its band, taken from char bound, then roster bound, then bought (`Calculator core/Domain model.md`, "Chests").

## The page

A row of tabs on top (local state in `Choices.vue`): "Bonus chests", this card, "Paradise" and "Custom" (both below), then a "More coming soon..." label that is not a tab. One card: a table of gate, cost, what it gives (icons and amounts, `others` on hover), worth (its header shows "Baseline = " the run without's average tradable gold used, `metric_per_plan[0]` as the job's `gold`, with what its leftovers are worth in brackets, plan 0 less plan 1 as on the calculator, on one line in a column at least 18rem wide, a spinner until run, "N/A" with no upgrades; every worth is against the user's plan's metric, not that gold) (rounded to whole gold, `signed_gold`; green positive, red negative, the main text colour for 0, a spinner (`Components/Common/Spinner.vue`) until run) and "Add to budget". Rows are grouped by raid, newest first (`raids_of`): by the item level of the raid's highest difficulty, whatever the character's, which is the order of release; ties keep the data's order. "Add to budget" reads "Added ✓" for two seconds after a click (keyed by group, title and option, since the job's hash changes with the budgets; by reward name on Paradise). The tables scroll inside their card on a narrow screen (`w-max min-w-full` in an `overflow-x-auto`), and the tab row clears the sidebar's menu button below 1201 px.

**Three raids** are shown and run, the newest; "Show one more raid" adds the next each press, for the visit (back to three on another character). Hidden raids are not run.

**Difficulties**: a raid's header has a button per difficulty (`difficulties`, lowest item level first; one above the character's item level is greyed), and only the rows of the one shown are listed and run. Which is shown (`shown_difficulty`): the one picked by hand, saved per character and raid in `profile.choice_levels` (by group name), while the raid still has it; else the highest the character's achieved item level (`ilevel`, gear only) reaches; else the lowest. A click is a pick, even on the one shown already, and there is no way back to automatic but picking the one wanted. A choice without a `difficulty` always shows.

**No upgrades** (the run without has no `upgrade_info`): nothing is run, every worth is "N/A" and a warning says to select some. Every run would be 0 otherwise, which reads as "costs nothing".

**A worth of 0 with upgrades** is real: under the default plan unused char-bound and roster-bound leftovers are worthless, gold included, so a chest paid from bound gold the upgrades would not use, giving materials they do not need, changes nothing.

**Add to budget** (`take_option`) adds what the option gives to the band's column (the char-bound budget for the bonus chests; free taps to the free-tap budget), unconverted, and takes a cost from that band, then roster bound, then tradable. It is disabled when the bands together cannot pay (`affordable`). Nothing marks a choice as taken.

## Paradise

The game offers three of a level's rewards and the player takes the best of them, so the tab shows every reward of one screen, best first, for the player to find the three offered in. Buttons pick the mode (Destiny, Netherworld), the item level (the choice's `difficulty`, shown as for raids, under the group "Paradise: Destiny") and the level (Base, Level 1 to 10). All three are saved in `profile.choice_levels`, the mode and level under "Paradise mode" and "Paradise level". A table of reward, what it gives, worth and "Add to budget"; the worth header has the **Wealth** checkbox (session state), which swaps every option for its `wealth` bag, so new runs and a new order. It is only there for a choice with `wealth` (Destiny); Netherworld has no Wealth and no additional rewards.

- In Destiny each option is the main reward plus the additional rewards, which every reward comes with; with Wealth, plus the Wealth rewards instead. In Netherworld it is the main reward alone. An option that is no material gives only those, so many options of one screen are the same payload and share one run; one that gives nothing at all (1580, or Netherworld without additional rewards) is worth 0 and not run.
- Sorted by worth, those not run yet last; rows move as runs come in.
- `Paradise.vue` queues its own runs (the run without first, then every option of the screen) and `Choices.vue` queues nothing while it is shown, since `queue_jobs` replaces the queue. It does not stop the jobs when it closes: `Choices.vue` does on leaving the page.
- A T4 character's Serca rewards keep only their shards, juice and gold (as for the raids).

## Custom

One choice the user builds, saved per roster in `roster_config.custom_choices` (by roster id, written on the first edit; `default_custom_choice` is two empty options until then), so every character of the roster sees and edits the same one. Buttons pick its band (char bound by default, roster bound, tradable), which is where the options give and what pays their costs, as for a bonus chest. A table of option (its name, editable), what it gives (per material an icon, an amount box and ×, then a "+ Material" select of every label), worth, "Add to budget" and "Remove" (not for the last option); "Add option" below. An amount is a whole number and a negative one is a cost; amounts of 0 are kept for editing but left out of the choice (`as_custom`), so an option of only 0s is empty, worth 0 and not run. Sorted best first like Paradise, so a row can move under the cursor as runs come in. Queues its own runs like Paradise. "Add to budget" is disabled for an empty option or one the bands cannot pay (`affordable`), and reads "Added ✓" keyed by `custom/<index>`.
