# Slot grid: what was tried

The cases behind the status rules of `Slot grid.md`. Only read this when trying something new there.


- **Never without a hover.** A slot that can only be irrelevant chests was irrelevant outright at first, and a "Fusion Material: Superior Abidos Fusion Material Chest" that scored 0.950 as a look-alike art on one recording was dropped unseen.
- **Irrelevant chests on the suite** (2026-10-10): 9 chest slots of the four recordings go from needing a hover to irrelevant, with 14 irrelevant chests read; every chest that counts is read as before and no slot's icon changes. 27 slots go from needing only their tradability to needing a hover, all on three templates: the two daily chests ("Daily Stone Chest III", "Daily Shard/Leapstone Chest III", each drawn like a "Romantic Tent Set") and the fusion material chest (drawn like the same chest of older tiers).
- A template shared with an irrelevant chest needs a hover: 29 templates were good without one before those rows were added.
