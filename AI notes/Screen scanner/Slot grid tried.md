# Slot grid: don't try these

Warnings behind the status rules of `Slot grid.md`. Only read this when trying something new there.

- **Don't make a slot that can only be irrelevant chests irrelevant outright.** A "Fusion Material: Superior Abidos Fusion Material Chest" that scored 0.950 as a look-alike art on one recording was dropped unseen; such a slot needs a hover.
- **Don't treat a template shared with an irrelevant chest as settled without a hover.** 29 templates were good without one before those rows were added; 27 slots went from needing only their tradability to needing a hover, all on three templates (the two daily chests, each drawn like a "Romantic Tent Set", and the fusion material chest, drawn like the same chest of older tiers).
