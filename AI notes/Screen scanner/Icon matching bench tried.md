# Icon matching bench: don't try these

Warnings from what the bench (`Icon matching bench.md`) measured on 2026-10-11 to decide what to change. The decoys that came out of it are in the scanner. Only read this when trying a new way of matching a slot. The numbers are on the seven lossless 1080p captures: 532 distinct slots with a template, about 1,400 without, from two rosters and four characters, so the hard cases are few.

- **Don't match by score alone.** The lowest true match is 0.946 and the highest slot with no template 0.966, so at a limit of 0.95 five slots are missed (3 kinds) and five let through (4 kinds). What gets through is another art that looks alike (a chest in another trim: `use_4_227` as `use_4_228`, `use_4_221` as `shop_icon_31`), the same art on another rarity's background (`use_2_7` rare against the epic template, 0.937 to 0.946), and a small art on a shared background (the fusion materials, whose art is 17% of the slot). This is why decoys exist.
- **Don't look for a better score.** 24 were tried (root mean square, art-weighted, art and background apart, worst cells, trimmed, luma and chroma apart, correlation, edges, softened, half size, and mixes). The best get 1 to 3 slots wrong at their best limit against 5, and 4 to 5 against 8 with the limit set on other captures. Weighting by the art alone is much worse: it throws the rarity away.
- **Don't soften or rescale to cut a true match's error.** A true match's error is fine detail: softening both sides with a 1-2-1 kernel halves it (8.3 levels to 4.6), but it helps look-alikes about as much; softening one side makes it worse; and no other way of scaling the template (box, bicubic, Lanczos, point-sampled, blurred, shifted by a quarter pixel) beats the bilinear one.
- **Don't lower the decoy threshold for a wider gap.** With the plain score the gap between the lowest true match (0.949) and the highest other slot is -0.017 with no decoys, +0.020 with the 129 that are 0.94 alike, +0.023 with 757 at 0.93, and +0.053 with 6,530 at 0.90. 0.93 is enough. No true match is vetoed at any of them.
- **Don't score art and background apart for the gap** (`art_bare_mad`, the worse of the two counting): it widens the gap (+0.084 at 0.90) but is worse on the lossy 1440p captures, where the plain score with decoys still has +0.089.
- **The result to keep beating**: with the scanner's own templates and its decoys, the lowest true match is 0.946 and the highest other slot 0.924, and a limit of 0.935 gets every slot right.
- **Don't trust the end records' labels for hard slots.** Never recognised, at 0.946 to 0.948, were "[Event] 1,000 Gold Bars" (`use_8_248@epic`), a honing material selection chest (`shop_icon_10804@epic`) and the Emergency Honing Materials Chest (`use_14_4@legendary`); a T3 "Oreha Fusion Material" (`use_9_71`, rare) read as the T4 "Fusion" at 0.966 in three captures. They are corrected in `LABELS` in `icon_data.py`. Chests of a template's art with an item level across them that is not read through were left out of the bench.
- The overlay measurements in `Pipeline tried.md` use the `c-*` captures.
