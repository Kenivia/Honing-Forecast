# Choices tried

Warnings for the Choices page (`Choices.md`): what failed or was ruled out.

- **Don't sign a worth before rounding it**: a worth under one gold showed "−0", and 0 was coloured as a loss. Round first.
- **Don't list every raid for every character**: a fresh 1640 character led with three Serca raids it cannot enter. List a raid only when its lowest difficulty is at or under the character's item level, whatever its tier.
- **Don't let a wide table be centred by the page on a narrow screen**: at 375 px its left side (the gate names) sat off-screen with no way to scroll to it. The table scrolls inside its card.
